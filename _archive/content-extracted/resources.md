# Resources

You will find here links to organisations that can provide further information about colorectal surgery in general and also sites that provide information about specific conditions.

The inclusion of these links here does not imply that they are endorsed by Dr Sutherland. It should be noted that for many issues the best evidence remains specialist opinion rather than definite facts. As such for many problems various opinions may be equally valid with no specific right or wrong answer.

### [Colorectal Surgical Society of Australia and New Zealand (CSSANZ)](http://www.cssanz.org)

a body established by colorectal surgeons in Australia and New Zealand to ensure the maintenance of the very highest standards of care by its members. It is the only recognised training organisation for colorectal surgeons in Australia.

The website contains information about colorectal surgery in Australia and several colorectal conditions.

### [American Society of Colon and Rectal Surgeons (ASCRS)](http://www.fascrs.org)

Equivalent to the CSSANZ the ASCRS website contains useful information for patients and doctors about colorectal conditions. The ASCRS publishes Diseases of the Colon and Rectum which is also the journal of the CSSANZ and the leading source of information for colorectal surgery worldwide.

### [Association of Coloproctology of Great Britain and Ireland (ACPGBI)](http://www.acpgbi.org.uk)

The ACPGBI is the UK equivalent of the CSSANZ. There is a great deal of shared training and information between the ACPGBI and the CSSANZ and indeed the ASCRS as well with colorectal trainees and surgeons visiting and collaborating extensively between these organisations to ensure our understanding of colorectal surgery is advanced throughout the world.

### [National Health and Medical Research Council (NHMRC)](http://www.nhmrc.gov.au/publications/synopses/cp106/cp106syn.htm)

The Australian Government’s National Health and Medical Research Council publishes guidelines for the management of a wide variety of conditions including Colorectal Cancer. These guidelines review the evidence relating to the prevention, early detection and management of colorectal cancer and act as a benchmark for clinical practice.

### [North Coast Area Health Service (NCAHS)](hhttp://www.ncahs.nsw.gov.au/services/results_detailed.php?serviceid=517)

The Coffs Harbour Base Hospital is part of the NCAHS. All of the surgeons at Coffs Harbour Surgical Group are accredited Visiting Medical Officers at the Base Hospital. For further information about the hospital and the Health Service follow this link.

### [Baringa Private Hospital](http://www.baringaprivate.com.au/)

Baringa Private Hospital is part of the Ramsay Health Care group and is the leading private hospital in the region. For further information about the hospital and the services it provides follow this link.

### [Pilonidal.org](http://www.pilonidal.org/)

This is a website for information on pilonidal sinu and related conditions. There is a section for medical professionals which gives details about modern approaches to surgery for this condition. In particular an article by Dr Paul Kitchen describes a highly successful technique. Dr Sutherland has worked with and been trained in this surgery by Dr Kitchen.

### [Australian Crohn’s and Colitis Association](http://www.acca.net.au/)

Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis are the main inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD). This support group provides information and assistance for people with IBD.

### [Australian Council of Stoma Associations](http://www.australianstoma.com.au/)

Some people may require a temporary or permanent stoma (bag) as part of their surgery for bowel resection.

### [Ostomy NSW Limited](http://www.ostomynsw.org.au/index.htm)

A self-help group for people who have had surgery resulting in creation of a stoma (bag)
